# Aura human character assets — v003

The Prince and Princess models use the MakeHuman Community base mesh, morph targets and default skeleton (CC0). Body proportions, facial target settings, procedural braided hair, glasses, materials, halos and animation clips were adapted for Aura.

- MakeHuman/MPFB: https://github.com/makehumancommunity/mpfb2
- Asset licensing: https://static.makehumancommunity.org/mpfb/faq/is_it_really_free.html
- Prince's tailored suit: **toigo_male_suit_3**, Margaret Toigo (MRT), CC0. Adapted fit and ivory material. https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/suits01.html
- Princess's gown: **elvs_goddess_dress1**, **Elvaerwyn**, **CC-BY** (license label supplied by the author). Adapted to the Princess body, widened skirt, ivory/gold material, and transferred skeleton weights. Source: https://www.makehumancommunity.org/node/2558 and https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/dress02.html

These are interim human characters, not exact likenesses of the supplied reference artwork. The GLBs retain weighted body, finger and facial bones. Authored clips: IdleBreathing, LookAround, Wave. Speech visemes, polished facial expressions, cloth simulation and final likeness sculpting are not complete.

The user's supplied Fischl STL print sculpt was inspected locally as a reference. Its geometry is not included in these downloadable character assets.
