ABOUT AURA

Mana is the power.
Aura is the presence.

AuraAI is being built as one adaptable intelligence that can understand context, express itself naturally, and work through clear human-approved boundaries.

THE IDEA

Technology should feel coherent, not fragmented.

Most digital work is split across tools, devices, conversations, and providers. Aura’s goal is to make those parts feel like one continuous relationship—without pretending that every system is the same or removing the permissions that keep people safe.

Aura is therefore organised as connected, independently permissioned systems: one to operate, one to learn, one to represent and reason, and one to connect modes and modular functions.

ONE CONNECTED INTELLIGENCE

Built to operate, learn, and evolve responsibly.

Four connected layers, each with a clear responsibility.

MME

Operates & expresses

Coordinates requests, memory, permissions, skills, rooms, voice, and visual presence.

MML

Learns & evaluates

Turns verified evidence into controlled, human-reviewed improvements.

MMM

Represents & reasons

Connects adaptable capabilities as one evolving machine intelligence.

MMF

Connects modes & functions

Brings Aura’s interaction modes and modular capabilities into the same connected architecture.

FOUNDING PRINCIPLES

Presence with responsibility.

Human-directed

People set the goal, approve meaningful access, and remain able to stop or change course.

Identity-aware

Aura distinguishes people, AI clients, roles, rooms, and devices instead of flattening them together.

Reviewable

Important actions and hand-offs should carry enough context to understand what happened and why.

Understandable

The visible experience stays calm and clear, while deeper records remain available to authorised systems.

DESIGNED WITH BOUNDARIES

Useful because you are in control.

Permissioned connectionsPrivacy-first trackingReviewable actionsHuman approval
PRIVACY APPROACH

Ask first. Collect less. Make access visible.

Aura’s visual prototype already treats camera movement tracking as optional, local, temporary, and immediately stoppable.

The same principle extends to future accounts and connections: access should be explicit, scoped, revocable, and never inferred from a browser session.
